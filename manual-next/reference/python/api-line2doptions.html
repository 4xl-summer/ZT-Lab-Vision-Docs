공개 타입

zvision.Line2DOptions: 패키지 __all__ 공개 이름입니다. 선언 출처: zvision._line_peaks_2d.Line2DOptions.

ROI-local central gradient, projection, angle/length/coverage and consensus limits.

Angles are radians. Polarity is 0=either, 1=rising, 2=falling along the
expected normal. Projection regions retain their physical stripe indices
after masking. Coverage is occupied stripes / inclusive stripe-index span.
max_lines=0 reports extracted peaks without fitting. min_contrast uses
half gradient magnitude; normalized contrast divides by mean intensity.
