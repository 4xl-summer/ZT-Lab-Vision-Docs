inspect_line_candidates 멤버와 선언

공개 타입

zvision.inspect_line_candidates: 패키지 __all__ 공개 이름입니다. 선언 출처: zvision._line_candidates.inspect_line_candidates.

Collect detected probe peaks and fit up to 32 lines, or zero for peaks only.

Threshold and prominence are applied before peak output; below-threshold peaks
are not returned. Candidates excluded by polarity are unconsidered. Candidates
actually submitted to fitting and left unassigned are considered outliers.
Sampling and results are ROI-local; this is not a global or vendor-equivalent search.
Shared fields selection=0, max_edges=1, min_found=min_inliers, min_arc_radians=0
and fit_method=1 are mandatory; unsupported alternatives are rejected.

측정선의 피크를 수집하고 선택적으로 선을 피팅합니다.

Python
inspect_line_candidates(image, spec, options, max_lines)
